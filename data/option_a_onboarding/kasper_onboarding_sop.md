# Kāsper client onboarding SOP (today: manual, about 6 hours per client across 3 people)

| # | Step | Owner | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deal marked Won in Pipedrive | Sasha | Day 0 |
| 2 | Create the ClickUp Space from the "Client template" (lists: Onboarding, Statics, Videos, UGC) | Squad Lead | Day 0 |
| 3 | Create the Slack shared channel `#ext-<client>` and invite client contacts; create internal `#int-<client>` | Squad Lead | Day 0 |
| 4 | Create the Drive folder in Client Folders: `01 Brand`, `02 Products`, `03 Briefs`, `04 Delivered`, `05 UGC` | Squad Lead | Day 0 |
| 5 | Send welcome email and intake form | Creative Lead | Day 0 |
| 6 | Kickoff call; transcript saved to the client folder | Creative Lead | Day 1–3 |
| 7 | Create the client in Studio: brand kit (colours, fonts, logo), import the product catalog | Squad Lead | Day 3 |
| 8 | Create the Client Brain doc from the intake, call and deal notes (every fact sourced) | Kāsper Brain bot | Day 3 |
| 9 | List open questions and conflicts; get answers from the client | Creative Lead | Day 3–5 |
| 10 | Creative strategy doc + first month's brief list | Creative Lead | Day 5 |
| 11 | First briefs go through the production gate into Queued | Squad Lead | Day 5 |
| 12 | Set up recurring ClickUp tasks: monthly report, weekly check-in | Squad Lead | Day 5 |
| 13 | Add the client to Client Health and Call Prep bots | Squad Lead | Day 5 |
| 14 | 2-week check-in call | Creative Lead | Day 14 |

Rule: nothing goes into production until step 9 is done (no open conflicts on claims, approver, brand colours or volume).
