# Halden Outdoor brand guidelines (v2.1, March 2024)
*Extract from the PDF in the client's asset folder.*

## Colour
- Forest green `#1F3D2B` (primary)
- Sand `#E8DCC4` (secondary)
- Ember orange `#D9622B` (accent, use sparingly)

## Type
Söhne Breit for headlines, Söhne for body. All-caps headlines are allowed up to 6 words.

## Logo
The stag mark with the wordmark. Keep clear space equal to the stag's antler height. Never put the logo on photos with busy skies.

## Photography
Real people, real weather, UK uplands. Dogs welcome. Our community loves them.

## Voice
Plain-spoken, a bit dry, never macho. Avoid "epic", "conquer" and "crush it".
